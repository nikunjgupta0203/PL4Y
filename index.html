WHAT IS PL4Y

Sports is more than a game.

It's the people you play with. The places you play. The matches you remember. The competition you chase. The communities you become part of.

PL4Y brings it together.

PL4Y is being built to help you:

  • Participate in events
  • Compete

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PL4Y? +
PL4Y is a modern sports technology and multi-sport participation platform. It is designed to bring your sports identity, regular play, match history, and local sports communities into one accessible, dedicated home.
Who is PL4Y for? +
PL4Y is for anyone who plays sports. Whether you pick up a racquet once a month on weekends or train and compete multiple times a week, PL4Y gives your sports journey a place to live. It is not limited to any single age group or skill bracket.
Which sports will PL4Y support? +
PL4Y is truly multi-sport from day one. We support Cricket, Football, Badminton, Pickleball, Tennis, Basketball, Running, Table Tennis, Volleyball, Squash, Swimming, Golf, Cycling, and are continuously adding more sports requested by our players.
When is PL4Y launching? +
PL4Y is launching soon. Early access invitations are being rolled out in waves to registered founding players first, allowing you to establish your profile and connect with initial local communities before public access opens.
Why should I register now? +
Registering now secures your early access invite, locks in your preferred sports profile identity, and connects you directly with the founding PL4Y community shaping sports participation in your city.
Is PL4Y only for events? +
No. PL4Y is a comprehensive sports identity and community platform. While we organize and partner with great competitions, tournaments, and social games, events are just one way our players participate. Your profile, stats, and player discovery remain with you continuously.
